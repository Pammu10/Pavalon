1. Generate the 5 character stills (Section A). Re-roll until you like each one.
2. Attach the approved stills as reference/ingredient images.
3. Generate the 12 video clips (Sections B & C), attaching the relevant character stills to each. Expect 3–5 attempts on the big battle clips (1, 7, 9, 10, 12).
4. If a clip drifts photorealistic, append: `flat painted cel shading, visible brush texture.` If a battle looks sparse, append: `epic scale, thousands of soldiers, extreme wide shot.`
